# Visual Design Reference

## Design character

Pharma Care presents as a calm, confident pharmacy workspace. Its visual language is practical and orderly, with a warm healthcare character: deep greens provide structure, pale mint surfaces soften the experience, and white panels keep information clear. The interface favors fast scanning over decoration. It feels suited to a busy counter where staff need to read names, quantities, totals, and status at a glance.

The main workspace is compact and information-rich, while the customer-facing display is spacious and reassuring. Across both, the same green identity, rounded shapes, and restrained use of color keep the experience recognizable.

## Color and themes

The light appearance is built from a very pale mint-green page background, clean white panels, and a dark leafy-green text color. A medium emerald provides the strongest brand emphasis for selected navigation, primary buttons, and important values. A brighter mint accent marks focus and small highlights. Muted green-gray text and soft green borders establish secondary hierarchy without creating harsh contrast.

The night appearance preserves the same identity with deep forest-green backgrounds, lighter green panels, pale text, and brighter mint accents. Borders and shadows become more subdued against the darker surfaces. It is a tonal adaptation rather than a separate visual style.

Red is reserved for destructive, urgent, or error-related emphasis. Amber signals caution or pending attention. Blue, purple, and teal appear in a few specialized areas, but they remain supporting accents rather than replacing the core green palette.

## Typography and hierarchy

The interface uses a clean, modern sans-serif style. Page titles and key totals are bold, often extra-bold, while descriptions, field labels, and metadata use smaller, calmer weights. Uppercase micro-headings with expanded letter spacing identify section labels and KPI categories.

Dense lists and tables use compact type sizes, but the hierarchy remains clear: column headings are muted and uppercase, row names are stronger, and numeric values are aligned and visually distinct. Prices and other amounts often use numeral styling that makes columns easier to compare. Urdu text uses a more calligraphic style that contrasts gently with the surrounding sans-serif interface.

## Shapes, surfaces, and depth

Cards and dialogs use rounded corners, usually with a modest radius. Standard controls have slightly tighter rounding, while prominent customer-facing elements and large modal surfaces feel softer. Borders are thin and low-contrast. Shadows are generally light and diffuse, providing separation without a floating, layered look.

White cards sit on pale mint or neutral backgrounds. Some prominent cards receive a faint surface gradient or a soft green glow on hover. Inputs and selects are clean rectangles with comfortable padding; their focus state gains a visible mint-green outline. Buttons are compact, solid, and clearly differentiated between primary green, outlined neutral, and red destructive treatments.

Tables use fine horizontal dividers, restrained row highlights, sticky-looking headings where space is limited, and generous horizontal scrolling when a data set is wider than its panel. Small rounded status chips provide quick visual classification.

## Shared workspace frame

The staff experience is arranged around a dark green vertical navigation rail, a persistent light toolbar, and a broad content canvas. The navigation rail has a deep layered-green treatment and light text. Section labels are small and subdued; the selected destination is brighter, bolder, and marked by a vivid mint edge. Icons are simple and compact. The rail can become a narrow icon strip, preserving the page’s useful width while reducing visual noise.

The top toolbar is shallow and translucent-looking, with a soft bottom edge and a slight blur effect. The brand mark and current section title sit to the left. Compact pill-shaped controls and small status indicators are grouped to the right. On smaller screens the header controls wrap, while the brand mark gives way to a simpler, more compact arrangement.

The content canvas follows a consistent rhythm: a page heading or toolbar, a small gap, then cards, tables, or split panels. Most pages use responsive grids that become denser on wide screens and stack into a single column on narrow ones. Important actions remain near the top of each page rather than being buried at the end of long content.

## Reusable visual patterns

- KPI tiles pair a small uppercase label with a large, bold value and sometimes a supporting icon or note.
- Filter rows combine search fields, select controls, and removable rounded chips; they wrap rather than becoming oversized.
- Data panels share pale borders, compact headings, small action menus, and subtle hover states.
- Empty and loading spaces use centered plain-language messages or muted skeleton bars rather than decorative illustrations.
- Status labels use compact rounded pills with green, amber, red, blue, or neutral coloring.
- Dialogs sit above a darkened, softly blurred backdrop. Their panels are white or theme-colored, with clear headers, close controls, and grouped actions.
- Focus, selection, and hover states are noticeable but restrained. Motion is brief and understated, with the option to reduce or turn off animation.

## Brand mark and small identity details

The brand mark combines a two-tone capsule-like symbol with a bold wordmark. In the staff navigation it appears in a horizontal arrangement against the dark rail; elsewhere it is sized to fit the surrounding space. The logo can appear as a static mark or with a gentle floating and rocking motion. The header may instead show a shop-specific image within a tidy, contained frame.

A small support-credit card uses the same dark-green identity, a compact centered layout, and bright green contact emphasis. It is visually subordinate to the main navigation and support content.

## Screen-by-screen visual reference

### Sign-in

The sign-in screen is centered and uncluttered. A branded heading sits above or within a white card with a thin border and soft shadow. The form is arranged vertically, with clearly labeled fields, comfortable control spacing, and a prominent green action. Blue-green accent details add a little contrast without changing the app’s wider mint-and-emerald character. The page gives the form room to breathe on narrow screens.

### Dashboard

The dashboard opens with a personal greeting and a compact summary label, with the day-closing action aligned on the opposite side when available. Beneath it, a broad grid of small KPI cards forms the main visual band. Each card pairs an icon tile and a short status tag with a strong numerical value. Green represents ordinary activity, amber highlights approaching attention, and red marks urgent stock conditions. The grid tightens across large screens and becomes a two-column arrangement on smaller widths.

A quick-actions area follows the metrics. It is designed as a concise set of direct, icon-led choices rather than a large illustration or marketing panel.

### Point of sale

The billing workspace uses a two-part desktop composition. The larger left side is a product-browsing area, beginning with compact tabs and a search-and-filter bar. The product catalog appears as a scrollable grid of small cards. Each card presents a bold medicine name, supporting strength or generic details, a price, a stock line, and a small add symbol. Hover and keyboard-selection states add a light green or blue-tinted edge and background.

The right side is a bordered cart panel with a compact header, customer selection, and a vertically scrollable item list. Each cart row groups the medicine name with price, quantity, unit, and small quantity controls. The lower portion is reserved for discount and payment details, a clear total, and a strong checkout action. On narrower screens, catalog and cart panels stack vertically instead of remaining side by side.

The barcode-oriented state gives the search area a stronger blue outline and a visible scan marker. Customer-display alternatives, when present, use small white option rows within the cart, with a dark circular logo badge for each choice. Errors and notices appear in compact full-width banners above the working area.

### Customer display

The customer display is intentionally more open than the staff workspace. A soft green-to-white background fills the page, with blurred mint shapes near the edges. A compact header places the logo on one side and a rounded live-status chip on the other. A narrow divider separates the header from the main content, and a centered footer completes the composition.

The welcome and thank-you states use a large simple symbol, generous whitespace, and centered green text. The order state places items inside a white rounded panel, with product details on one side and aligned amounts on the other. Subtotals and payment figures sit in a pale mint summary area. Alternative products appear as white cards with a bold name, muted supporting details, and a softly tinted price badge. This screen uses larger type and calmer spacing than the staff pages so it can be read from across a counter.

### Products and stock inventory

The product-management view starts with a compact title and a toolbar of search, add, import/export, and additional actions. Filters appear as small rounded controls and chips, with a slim row of summary figures below. Product records are shown either in a dense table or a responsive card layout. The table emphasizes product identity, classification, stock, price, expiry, and compact row actions; on smaller screens, a stacked card treatment keeps the same fields readable.

Stock inventory uses the same visual foundation, with KPI cards, segmented category tabs, filter controls, stock bars, expiry chips, and compact movement or batch tables. Green bars indicate healthy levels, amber indicates low levels, and red signals empty or urgent levels. Expiry labels follow a similar green, amber, red, and neutral sequence. Bulk actions appear as a floating, sticky-looking strip near the lower edge when relevant.

Forms and product details use spacious rounded dialogs. Product details are arranged as a two-column definition list on wider screens and a single column on narrow screens. Product entry groups related fields into a grid, with unit details collected in a separate bordered section. Import review uses a scrollable list of row cards, with validation notes and clear footer actions.

### Categories and master data

This screen uses a small title block followed by a horizontal bar of rounded section tabs, search, sort, and refresh controls. Similar-name notices sit in a pale amber panel. The main records appear in a wide, compact table with small colored markers, strong names, count and value columns, stock-health indicators, and actions aligned on the right. Empty states are centered in a quiet panel. Editing, merging, and deletion use focused dialogs with concise forms and clearly separated confirmation actions.

### Customer ledger

The ledger begins with a search field and a green add-customer button. Customer summaries are displayed in a responsive card grid. Each card pairs a round initial badge with the customer name and phone, while the due amount is aligned to the opposite side. A thin divider separates this identity area from the credit-limit note and small reminder/payment actions.

Selecting a customer opens a right-side statement drawer with a strong emerald header, a circular initials badge, customer details, and a prominent balance. A pale-green action strip sits below it, followed by a scrollable ledger table. Payment and new-customer dialogs are compact white panels with clear headings, stacked fields, and a green primary action.

### Sales history and analytics

Sales history is organized as a chronology, with a compact summary strip and grouped sale sections. Day or period headings use bold text and small count/value markers; individual sales are presented as readable rows or cards with a strong invoice identifier, softer metadata, and visible totals. Filter chips and print or report controls stay near the top.

The analytics view uses a more spacious dashboard arrangement: a row of KPI tiles, period-selection chips, a broad trend visualization, and a lower group of smaller summary cards. The chart panel carries most of the page width, while compact supporting cards provide a clear visual finish. Green is the primary data color, with limited blue, amber, and red accents for comparison or caution.

### Suppliers and purchasing

Supplier management uses a compact heading and action area above a search-and-filter region and a tabular supplier list. Names are visually dominant; contact details and secondary information are muted. Add and edit forms are presented as clear card-like panels or dialogs with consistent field spacing.

The stock-purchase screen is arranged around a product-selection area and a purchase cart. Search and supplier controls sit close to the top. Product choices appear in compact cards or rows, while selected items form a scrollable list with batch, expiry, unit, quantity, and price details. Totals and payment information are grouped into a distinct summary panel, with a strong final action below. Purchase history uses a narrow heading with export and refresh buttons, followed by three slim summary tiles, a dense filter toolbar, and a wide table. Payment types use green and amber pills. Selecting a record opens a large detail dialog with supplier information, item table, totals, notes, and print or close actions.

### Cash book and day summary

The cash book places a short heading and a red-tinted expense action above a row of four slim KPI tiles. Inflows use green, outflows use red, the expected drawer amount stays neutral, and shift status is shown as a small blue or green badge. The lower area divides into a broad transaction table and a narrower cash-counting or shift-close panel. The table is compact, with time and transaction labels set smaller than the description and amount.

The day-summary dialog is narrow and centered. It stacks a date heading, grouped sales and outflow breakdowns, a teal profit strip, and a larger pale-green cash total. A cash-count field and difference line follow. Print, share, and close actions form a compact row at the bottom.

### Staff, permissions, and activity records

Staff management uses a clear page heading, a compact notice area, and a table of team members. Role and account-state labels appear as small chips; permission details are arranged in a structured grid or matrix rather than long prose. Add/edit and account-access dialogs follow the shared white rounded-panel pattern.

The activity archive places filters above a wide history table. Rows use subdued date and actor details, with stronger entity names and compact state chips. Restore actions are visually distinct but remain secondary to the record itself. Print-request screens use the same table language: personal requests emphasize status and timestamps, while the administrative review surface adds requester and receipt-detail columns with clear approval or rejection emphasis.

### Reports and controlled-record register

The print-sales-report panel is a single white rounded card with a thin heading area, period chips, a paper-size selector, and a green print button. Small invoice and medicine counts appear beneath the controls. The resulting print layouts are monochrome and document-like, with strong headings, simple rules, and carefully aligned totals.

The specialized controlled-record register is visually distinct from the ordinary green workspace. A pale red compliance banner with a shield symbol introduces the page; a search and classification row follows, then a wide table. Red and purple pills distinguish record classes, while patient, prescriber, date, and quantity details use compact text and numeral alignment. Its alert treatment is intentionally more serious than the rest of the app.

### Settings and printed-memo preview

Settings is a centered, moderately narrow column of white cards. The shop-profile card begins with a small icon and title, followed by logo preview and upload controls, then a two-column contact section that stacks on narrow screens. Memo options, footer details, and reminder text are presented in similar grouped panels with compact labels and straightforward toggles.

The template preview is a large dialog with a dark header and a two-column interior on wide screens. The left column groups paper-size choices, template choices, a specification card, and apply/cancel actions. The right side is a scrollable light-gray preview area containing a centered white receipt. On smaller screens the controls move above the preview. The different receipt styles range from compact, dashed-rule thermal slips to a more spacious invoice with thin borders and card-like totals. All use black-on-white print styling, clear item rows, and strong total emphasis.

### Help and support

The support page uses a centered content width and a simple title at the top. A support card combines a compact credit panel and a wrapping row of contact actions. Below it, a larger guide card contains bordered, expandable help rows with bold summaries and muted explanatory text. A small version note sits centered at the bottom. The page remains calm and open, with no dense data table.

### Alerts, scanner, label, and return dialogs

The stock-alert control is a small bell button with a count badge and a separate tiny unseen marker. Its panel is a white, bordered popover with a sticky header and grouped low-stock and out-of-stock lists. Each alert row is compact, showing a bold product name and a muted stock detail. The panel becomes full-width near the top edge on narrow screens.

The scanner test appears as a focused dialog with a clear title, scanning-status details, and a large, easy-to-read result area. Barcode-label printing uses a wide modal with size and copy controls beside a preview sheet of labels. The label designs are high contrast and compact, with the medicine name and barcode as their visual focus.

The returns panel uses a bottom-sheet shape on small screens and a centered dialog on wider screens. Its header and content are separated by thin borders. Sale lookup and item rows appear first; the approval section, when shown, uses stacked bordered cards and neutral form controls. Green marks completion, amber highlights a caution, and red is reserved for errors. The dialogs remain scrollable within the viewport rather than stretching the page.

## Responsive and motion behavior

Responsive layouts preserve the same hierarchy while reducing the number of columns. Grids become stacked, wide tables scroll horizontally or shift into cards, headers wrap, and dialog controls move above their previews. The interface generally maintains compact vertical spacing, but the customer display and sign-in page deliberately use more open space.

Motion is brief: cards can lift slightly, icons can shift a little, and small indicators may pulse. It is never the main visual feature. The animated logo is the most distinctive movement. Reduced-motion preferences and an in-app animation setting allow the interface to remain still.

## Overall impression

The design is a coherent, operations-first pharmacy interface: mint backgrounds and emerald structure establish a reassuring identity; bold, compact typography makes dense information scannable; and restrained borders, rounded cards, and modest shadows keep the many screens from feeling crowded. Specialized customer and compliance surfaces adjust the tone for their audience while remaining recognizably part of the same visual system.
